# Jaya Ayurveda — Product

## The problem

Chronic lifestyle conditions are not caused by ignorance. Most people know they should walk, sleep earlier, and eat better. They fail because advice arrives **at the wrong time, in the wrong amount, with no feedback loop**.

Existing wellness products make the same structural mistake: they are *destinations*. You must remember to open them, remember what to log, and then interpret the result yourself. Adherence collapses within weeks.

## The inversion

> A wellness app waits for you. Jaya comes to you.

Jaya emits roughly **20 prompts per day**, each one:

1. **Timed to your actual day** — derived from your profession, not a generic 9-to-5.
2. **Filtered through your constitution** — Vata, Pitta or Kapha determines the instruction, not just the label.
3. **A single question with two or three taps** — confirm, correct, or skip.
4. **Consequential** — every answer changes tomorrow's meals, plan and progress card.

There is no dashboard to study. There is no streak to defend. There is one calm instruction and one honest answer.

## A day, in practice

| Time | Prompt | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|
| 06:30 | Wake up — Brahma Muhurta | The clearest window of the day; anchoring wake time fixes the sleep cycle downstream |
| 06:30 | Pranayama and meditation | 10 minutes raises vagal tone; the body's own brake pedal |
| 08:00 | Breakfast: cooling coconut rice pudding | Pitta needs cooling, moist food; the specific dish rotates daily |
| 10:30 | Water check | Dehydration is routinely mistaken for hunger and fatigue |
| 12:30 | Lunch: kitchari with coconut | Agni peaks at midday; this should be the largest meal |
| 13:00 | Ten-minute walk | Flattens the post-lunch glucose curve |
| 15:00 | A small pause, on purpose | Profession-matched humour — the only prompt with no health goal |
| 16:30 | Afternoon snack (if hungry) | Kapha is advised to skip it; Vata is not |
| 19:30 | Dinner: mung dal soup | Lighter than lunch, three hours before sleep |
| 20:00 | Shall we plan tomorrow? | Meals + shopping list while shops are open |
| 21:30 | Wind down | Screens off, oil the feet, dim the lights |
| 22:30 | Shall I close the day? | Progress card: achieved, improve, tomorrow |

## Personalisation layers

1. **Constitution (dosha)** — 15-question assessment → Vata/Pitta/Kapha composition. Drives food, movement, sleep advice, humour style and goal eligibility.
2. **Profession** — 15 profiles with their own wake/sleep times, meal times, break cadence, screen-time limit, work note and family window.
3. **Region** — six Indian regions with local grains, vegetables, fruits and specials; seasonal produce rotates by month.
4. **Body** — age, gender, weight, height → WHO BMI, healthy weight band, automatic goal.
5. **Behaviour** — every confirmation or skip feeds the next day's plan and the nightly review.

## Daily variation

Content is rotated by day-index, not randomly:

- meal suggestions per slot
- dosha-specific micro-tips (8 per dosha)
- daily quote and encouragement
- profession-matched humour (technical / motivational / wholesome / philosophical)
- seasonal produce and holiday awareness

So the same user does not see the same breakfast, tip or joke two days running.

## What Jaya deliberately is not

- Not a diagnostic tool, and not a substitute for a physician.
- Not a calorie counter or a food database.
- Not a social network and not a competitive leaderboard.
- Not a wearable-data pipeline — it asks the user, rather than inferring silently.

## Success signals

- Prompt confirmation rate (target: >55% of surfaced prompts answered)
- Evening plan acceptance rate (the single highest-leverage habit)
- 7-day retention by profession cohort
- BMI trajectory for users who entered outside the healthy band
